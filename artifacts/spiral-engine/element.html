The Spiral Engine

A game where everyone wins.

Three players. Seventy-four cards. One shared objective and an unreasonable number of paths to get there.

WHAT IS THIS FOR?

The Spiral Engine is a cooperative game for practicing a different kind of strategic thinking.

Each round begins with a shared objective. The objective might be consequential, ridiculous, or both. That isn't really the point. The point is what happens between you as you try to create a way forward together.

You make a move. Someone else changes what is possible. A constraint becomes useful. A bad idea opens another path. Something that seemed essential becomes unnecessary. There is no strategy waiting to be discovered or correctly executed. Strategy is created through attention, response and relationship.

The questions can be silly because the thinking doesn't have to be. By lowering the stakes of what you are solving, the game gives you room to notice how you create strategy: what you assume, what you overlook, what you protect, when you adapt, and what becomes possible when nobody needs to win alone.

In the Good for Nothing Universe, that practice matters. Many of the things worth making cannot be planned into existence by a single intelligence. They require us to become better at noticing conditions, working with what is already present, and allowing a direction to change as other people change it.

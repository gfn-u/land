The Spiral Engine

01 / Welcome

A game where everyone wins.

Three players.
Seventy-four cards.
One shared objective and an unreasonable number of paths to get there.

The Spiral Engine invites you to an upside-down game where victory requires no losers. Its rules and contents create a universe in which players gather resources, complete essentials, and work towards a winning state together.

The winning condition requires all three players to hold a particular set of resources. How you achieve this is largely your business—and, sooner or later, everybody else's.

You can cooperate or compete, be concerned with efficiency or the lack thereof. You may collaborate in unexpected ways or try to screw one another over from the start. The game doesn't particularly mind. Don't forget, each move has consequences. The outcome is set. How you chose to play will be revealed.

The board can also be explored as a strategy puzzle. Its arrangements, relationships, and possible sequences offer plenty to investigate before—or between—games.

In this GfNU edition, every card includes a question and a small impulse for your enjoyment and benefit. Some are curious, some absurd, and some unexpectedly personal. You can explore them, ignore them, or get wonderfully distracted—the game goes on regardless of how you engage with its contents.

One last observation: The GfNU noticed you hate Monopoly. It's curious how you choose to play when the winning conditions are different—if there's even the patience to compete in a game with such conditions.

How to play

Shared objective. Each player must finish with three Level 2 tokens, each containing a Level 1 token, and one additional Level 1 token. Together these represent all six token S-categories. The tokens that require suit cards must be complete. Everyone wins only when all three players meet this condition.

Suit cards. The 52 smaller cards carry a suit (♠ ♥ ♣ ♦) and an S-category (S1–S9). Land on an unreserved suit card to reserve it for your hand. A card's suit determines whether it belongs in a required set; its S-category identifies the card independently of its color.

Essentials. The 22 numbered larger cards also have S-categories. Each essential specifies an exact set of suits. Without the required resources, it is simply a space you can land on. Each essential can be completed once.

Level 1 token. Complete a Level 1 essential while holding its required four suit cards. These cards are associated with that token.

Level 2 token. Complete a Level 2 essential using the specified Level 1 token and two additional suit cards of the required suits. The Level 1 token remains part of Level 2. Its original four suit cards are available to use again. This works identically in every play mode.

Giving. Once a player has the exact complete set, they must reach the center by ordinary movement to enter giving status. They can then give resources by landing on one of their own reserved spaces. Give either a suit card or a token to another player. Giving a token leaves its associated suit cards with the giver, who can distribute them separately. The recipient must obtain the required suits for that token to count as complete. A given reserve transfers to the recipient. If the giver loses their complete set, they return to gathering and must complete the set and revisit the center to give again.

Turns. Roll the six-sided die, then choose a highlighted legal space. Your selected mode controls how much movement freedom and board information is shown. The table log counts the team's completed turns.
